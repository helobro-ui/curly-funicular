Spiral Abyss

Endgame Content
The Spiral Abyss is the hardest content in the game. Floors 9–12 require two separate teams. Prioritize shield breakers against armored enemies and bring Anemo support for grouping.

Artifact Farming

Character Building
Farm the right artifact domain for your main DPS. Aim for 2-piece + 2-piece bonus first, then transition to 4-piece sets. Prioritize CRIT Rate, CRIT DMG, and ATK% substats.

Domain Strategies

Resource Optimization
Always use Condensed Resin to double your domain rewards. Bring an Anemo character to group enemies for faster kills. Clear under 30 seconds for optimal runs.

Boss Farming

Weekly & World Bosses
Weekly bosses reset Monday at 4 AM. Fight them early in the week. World bosses cost 40 Resin — use Condensed Resin when available. Know each boss's elemental weakness.

Elemental Reactions

Vaporize
🔥 Pyro + 💧 Hydro
The strongest reaction. Hydro → Pyro gives 1.5× multiplier; Pyro → Hydro gives 2×. Build around this for massive burst damage.
Melt
❄ Cryo + 🔥 Pyro
Cryo → Pyro gives 2× damage; Pyro → Cryo gives 1.5×. Ganyu's charged shots melting are a meta staple for insane single-target damage.
Overloaded
⚡ Electro + 🔥 Pyro
Causes an AoE Pyro explosion. Knocks lighter enemies away — great for clearing but can scatter enemies. Scales with Elemental Mastery.
Superconduct
❄ Cryo + ⚡ Electro
Reduces Physical RES by 40% for 12 seconds. Core reaction for any Physical DPS like Eula. Always pair with an Electro support.
Freeze
💧 Hydro + ❄ Cryo
Immobilizes enemies. Frozen enemies take 200% damage from Shatter. Great for Ayaka, Ganyu, and Kokomi teams. Makes Spiral Abyss much easier.
Swirl
🌀 Anemo + Any Element
Spreads and amplifies elements. Key use: trigger 4-piece Viridescent Venerer to shred elemental RES by 40%. Every team wants an Anemo support.
Quicken / Aggravate
🌿 Dendro + ⚡ Electro
Quicken creates a Quicken aura. Follow-up Electro triggers Aggravate (+15% flat DMG bonus). Dendro + Electro teams are among the strongest in the game.
Bloom / Burgeon
🌿 Dendro + 💧 Hydro
Creates Dendro Cores that explode after 6 seconds. Burgeon (adding Pyro) explodes them instantly in an AoE. Nilou's Bloom team is S-tier for crowd AoE.
Crystallize
🪨 Geo + Any Element
Creates elemental shields. Geo characters like Zhongli provide near-unbreakable shields through crystallize. Great for keeping your DPS alive.

Pro Tips for New Players

01

Don't spread your resources thin

Level one main DPS to 90 before touching anyone else. Talent materials and mora are scarce — focus on one carry first, then build supports around them.

02

Always run 4 elements in your team

Mono-element teams miss out on powerful reactions. Even adding a single Anemo character (Kazuha, Sucrose, or free Sayu) unlocks 4-piece Viridescent Venerer, shredding enemy resistance by 40%.

03

Save your Primogems for limited banners

Standard banner characters (Diluc, Mona, Keqing, etc.) can be obtained from the standard banner or events over time. Limited 5-stars like Kazuha and Hu Tao only return occasionally — save for those.

04

Learn enemy attack patterns

Dodging at the right moment triggers a dash with i-frames (invincibility frames), making you immune to damage. Practice against Ruin Guards and weekly bosses — it's better than any shield.

05

Elemental Mastery matters more than you think

EM directly boosts reaction damage. For supports that trigger Swirl, Bloom, or Vaporize, stacking EM (800–1000+) can dramatically outperform raw ATK builds. Kazuha and Nahida love EM.

06

Aim for 1:2 CRIT Rate to CRIT DMG ratio

The golden rule: for every 1% CRIT Rate, aim for 2% CRIT DMG. A 70% CRIT Rate / 140% CRIT DMG stat line is a great target. Use the CRIT circlet artifact to hit these numbers efficiently.
